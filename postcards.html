Postcards

This set of postcards was designed for El Yunque National Park in southeastern Puerto Rico. The cards feature Taíno petroglyphs, culturally significant symbols of Puerto Rican heritage. Each design includes both an original and a modernized interpretation of the petroglyph, along with information explaining their significance.

An Instagram-format version of the series was also created, including an animated cover image.
