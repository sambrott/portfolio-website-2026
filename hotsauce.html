Hot Sauce

This set of three hot sauce flavors celebrates the Indigenous culture of Puerto Rico. Each bottle features a symbol inspired by Taíno petroglyphs, drawing from the visual language of the island’s native people of the El Yunque region.

The flavors Guava, Papaya, and Passionfruit reflect ingredients commonly found across the island. Each label includes historical context on the Taíno culture and explains each petroglyph. The set includes custom 3D-printed charms designed for the products.
